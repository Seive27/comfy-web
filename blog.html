Deterministic rendering: why your UI should never drift

Non-deterministic output is the silent killer of trust in AI-generated interfaces. When the same brief produces different layouts on subsequent runs, users stop relying on the tool. They regenerate until something looks right, then edit the rest by hand. That is not automation. That is roulette. Deterministic rendering locks every output to its input so regeneration is a reload, not a gamble.
